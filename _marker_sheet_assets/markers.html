Setup steps

  1. Print this sheet at 100 % scale. Verify with the ruler check below — the bar should measure exactly 5 cm.
  2. Cut along the four dashed boxes.
  3. Lay the four markers face-up on the bed mat or floor in the rectangle shown to the left, IDs in the labelled positions.
  4. Patient lies supine inside the rectangle, head at the 0/1 end, arms by their sides.
  5. Take one overhead photo so all four markers are clearly visible — phone on a chair / tripod aimed straight down works fine.
  6. Upload to the anthroheight UI; with all four markers detected the system computes the bed-plane scale automatically.
